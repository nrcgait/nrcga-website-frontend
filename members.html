Join the NRCGA Community

NRCGA membership brings together excavators, utility operators, locators, and other stakeholders committed to damage prevention and public safety across Nevada. As a member, you'll have access to training, networking opportunities, and the ability to shape the future of damage prevention in our state.

Member Benefits

🤝

Networking

Connect with industry professionals at meetings, events, and committee sessions.

📊

Industry Data

Access to DIRT reports, damage statistics, and industry insights.

🗳️

Voting Rights

Participate in NRCGA governance and help shape policies and programs.

How to Join

1

Contact Us or Complete an Application

Reach out to NRCGA to express your interest in membership and learn more about the process. You can also complete an application form and we will get back to you with more information.

2

Review & Approval

Your application will be reviewed by the NRCGA board for approval.

3

Welcome!

Once approved, you'll receive membership information and can start participating in NRCGA activities.

Membership types

Choose the membership type that best fits your organization when you apply.

Stakeholder

Voting membership for organizations actively involved in damage prevention across Nevada.

Associate

Non-voting membership for organizations that support NRCGA's mission and programs.

Affiliate

Affiliate membership for partners and related organizations aligned with damage prevention.
